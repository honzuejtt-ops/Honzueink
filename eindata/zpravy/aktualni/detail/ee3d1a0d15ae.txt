Plugin extensions
We’re opening the platform we use to build ChatGPT features so developers can create their own experiences within ChatGPT. Plugin extensions let you give your plugin a home in the sidebar and build interactive panels where people can work alongside the conversation. We’re making it easier to build plugins and help people discover them. Plugin Creator helps you build your plugin while a redesigned submission flow provides clearer feedback. Pages
Pages are a new type of document, built for human and agent collaboration. Anything you can do in ChatGPT you can do on a page: write, research, generate charts, create images, or visualize information. Create a page in a conversation, then invite your team to contribute ideas and feedback.
Soon you’ll be able to create interactive slides with ChatGPT and your team, from a conversation or your own template. Multiple teammates and agents can edit the deck at the same time and leave comments. Present in ChatGPT or export to PowerPoint or Google Slides with formatting intact.
Now you can bring colleagues together as a team in ChatGPT to share pages, slides, plugins, and spreadsheets. You can also delegate recurring work such as weekly project updates to team tasks. ChatGPT uses connected tools to gather information and take action on a schedule or in response to events like a new email or Slack message. Teammates can refine the instructions together as priorities change.
We’re making it easier to work with ChatGPT where team conversations happen. Mention @ChatGPT in a channel, thread or DM to get help with your team’s work. It can use tools connected by your admin or your own tools with your permissions. Teammates can add context and refine the results in the same conversation without needing an individual ChatGPT license.
The Meetings plugin takes notes for you and saves personalized summaries with action items in ChatGPT Space. Keep them private or share them with your team, then ask ChatGPT to update a project plan or draft a follow-up. Audio is deleted once your notes are ready and can’t be accessed or replayed.
